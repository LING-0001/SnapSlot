# Desktop demonstration assets

These third-party operating-system materials illustrate the host platforms in the
Snapshot Pin website demonstration. They are not Snapshot Pin brand assets and
are not covered by the repository's MIT source-code license. Apple and Microsoft
retain rights in their respective wallpaper artwork, app icons and trademarks.

- `macos-sonoma.jpg`: macOS Sonoma system wallpaper, converted from the
  system-supplied `Sonoma.heic`. Resized to 2000px and JPEG encoded; no recoloring.
- `macos-sonoma-dusk.jpg`: the second, blue/green system-supplied Sonoma HEIC
  frame, resized to 2000px and JPEG encoded without recoloring.
- `windows-11-bloom.jpg`: Windows 11's original light Bloom wallpaper (`img0.jpg`).
  Asset mirror: https://github.com/bartekl1/windows-ui-assets/blob/main/Wallpapers/Windows%2011/Desktop/Windows/img0.jpg
  Microsoft's description of the original work:
  https://blogs.windows.com/windowsexperience/2021/10/06/windows-11-blossoms-with-bloom-a-new-symbol-for-a-new-operating-system/
  Resized to 2000px and JPEG encoded; no recoloring.
- `coastline.jpg`: system-supplied Big Sur Coastline thumbnail, JPEG encoded.
- `sonoma-horizon.jpg`: system-supplied Sonoma Horizon still, resized to 2000px
  and JPEG encoded.
- `finder.png`, `safari.png`, `notes.png`, `textedit.png`: system-supplied macOS
  application icons, converted to 96px PNGs for the demonstration Dock.

The demonstration remains an interactive website; these are OS visual assets,
not evidence of native-client behavior or an endorsement from either vendor.
